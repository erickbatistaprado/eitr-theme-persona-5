# Persona 5 artwork

The 7 wallpapers are art inspired by Persona 5, shared here as fan content for
personal desktops, free of charge. Each picture belongs to its artist, and the work it
shows belongs to Atlus and Sega. This theme is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of them.

- `01-joker.jpg`: Joker; artist unknown
- `02-phantom-thieves.jpg`: The Phantom Thieves; artist unknown
- `03-take-your-heart.jpg`: Joker in the red; artist unknown
- `04-tokyo.jpg`: Tokyo; artist unknown
- `05-tactica.jpg`: Persona 5 Tactica; artist unknown
- `06-futaba.jpg`: Futaba Sakura; artist unknown
- `07-the-thieves-hideout.jpg`: Makoto, Haru, Futaba and Ann; artist unknown

If one of these is yours, open an issue to be credited by name or to have it removed.

The palette is drawn from these pictures: black background, calling-card white text, Phantom Thieves red accent, Joker red, Palace green, Skull yellow, Tactica blue, Velvet violet and Mementos teal.
